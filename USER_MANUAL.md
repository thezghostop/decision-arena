## What Is Decision Arena?

Decision Arena is an AI-powered decision simulation platform. You describe a decision you're facing, and a panel of expert AI agents debates it from multiple angles â exposing blind spots, stress-testing assumptions, and surfacing risks you might have missed.

## Getting Started

### 1. Sign Up
Visit the app and sign in with your Google or email account (powered by Clerk).

### 2. Start a New Debate
On the home page, click **Start a Debate** and fill in:
- **Your Question** â the decision or dilemma you want debated
- **Category** â Business, Technology, Policy, Personal, or Other
- **Debate Mode** â choose how many perspectives you want

### 3. Watch the Debate Unfold
The panel of AI experts assembles and debates live. You'll see:
- Each expert's opening statement, cross-examination, and closing argument
- A **Scoreboard** showing each expert's scores
- A **Final Verdict** with the top recommendation

### 4. Ask the Panel
During or after the debate, use **Ask the Panel** to inject your own question.

### 5. Export the Report
Once the debate completes, click **Export Report** to download a full PDF summary.
